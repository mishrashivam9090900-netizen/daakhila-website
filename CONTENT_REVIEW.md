# Content review before launch

These statements are intentionally retained as general guidance and must be checked against the current official brochure before publication:

- Home-state, other-state, category and NIOS eligibility descriptions.
- The Delhi/JAC Delhi example and every state/counselling label.
- Document checklist items and certificate validity requirements.
- Any future cutoff, rank, seat-share, date, scholarship or loan information.
- The phrase “Live” for a state or course once current-year data is added.
- Draft privacy and terms pages; a founder and qualified Indian legal/privacy reviewer must approve them.

The site does not publish current cutoffs, dates, seat counts, guaranteed outcomes, testimonials or mentor identities.
